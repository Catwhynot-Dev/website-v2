My Academic Journey

Published on October 26, 2023

Welcome to a personal look into my academic journey as a high school junior. My coursework this year has been both challenging and incredibly rewarding. From the complex equations in AP Chemistry to the intricate systems of the human body in Honors Physiology, I've found a lot to be excited about. I'm also currently enrolled in AP Government and CP English III, which have helped me grow my critical thinking and writing skills.

My goal is to pursue a career in nursing, and these courses are laying a strong foundation for that. They're helping me not only understand the scientific principles behind health and medicine but also develop the discipline and dedication needed for the field.

This journey isn't just about grades; it's about building a passion for learning and a set of skills that will serve me for years to come. I'm excited to see where it leads, especially as I start to look more closely at colleges like Ohio State and Miami University.
